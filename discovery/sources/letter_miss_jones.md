# Letter — Miss Jones, Class 2B, Localsville Primary School

Localsville Primary School
Class 2B

8 January 2026

Dear Localsville Public Library,

I am writing as the class teacher for 2B to ask whether the library might
add Frank Murr's *What a Mess* books to its collection. Several of my
pupils already hold library cards and borrow from you each week, and I am
certain these titles would be borrowed steadily.

I recommend them from experience. At my previous school, HobKnob Private
in Upper Kensingtonsenfrengen, the *What a Mess* books were quite the
success at encouraging the children to keep reading once they had gone
home for the day — the reluctant readers especially. A child who will not
open a book at their desk will happily take Frank Murr home.

I do not use the borrowing system myself, of course; my interest is simply
that the children in my class have good books within reach. If a few
copies could be obtained, I would gladly point the parents of 2B towards
them.

With thanks,

Miss Jones
Class 2B, Localsville Primary School
