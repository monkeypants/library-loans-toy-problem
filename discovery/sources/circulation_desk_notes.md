# Circulation Desk — Goals and Pain Points

## Goals and objectives

The circulation desk records every book that goes out on loan, tracking
which member holds it and when it is due back. Staff want to issue a loan
in a single step at the counter.

Each physical volume carries its own barcode, and the desk scans the
barcode of the copy being borrowed rather than the catalogue record.

A loan is out until the volume comes back; once its due date has passed
while still out it is flagged overdue, and it closes as either returned or
lost.

## Pain points and frustrations

Members frequently ask whether a title is available, but staff have no
quick way to search the catalogue from the desk. Locating a member's
current loans is slow and manual.

When every copy of a title is already out, members ask to be put on a
waiting list for the next one returned.
